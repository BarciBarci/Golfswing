Golf Swing Analysis plays back a swing video in slow motion (down to
0.1x) and lets you draw lines and circles directly on the video to check
your posture, spine angle, head position or swing path.

Features:
- Open a video from your device (MP4, WebM, MOV) or a .glf project
- Trim the swing: cut off the start and the end before analyzing
- Playback speed continuously adjustable from 0.1x to 1x
- Single-frame stepping - tap once for one frame, press and hold to step
  through frame by frame
- Draw lines and circles in 9 colors directly on the video; move whole
  shapes or single grips afterwards
- Restrict each line/circle to a time window, adjust stroke width,
  toggle visibility, undo
- Save your work as a .glf project (video plus all overlays in one file)
  and reopen it at any time; export/import overlays as JSON
- Works fully offline - videos and projects never leave your device.
  No Internet permission, no trackers, no analytics.
